NPC Generator

Notes about using the NPC Generator

  • Use the Stat Tracker tab to plan your build and spend BP, then click Load into Generator to carry Name, Attributes, Skills, HP, DP, and MP over to the Stat Block Generator tab.
  • View Summary opens a side panel with your tracked stats and BP features so you can reference it while working in either tab.
  • If you check Mythic, do not adjust HP/DP/MP manually, since they will be auto-scaled when you generate.
  • The Mythic checkbox will also automatically add all Mythic traits to the stat block.
  • Opposing modifiers for skills are calculated automatically.
  • Adding an Ability Standard Action will auto-add the Charging quick action.
  • Save JSON / Load JSON saves your Tracker and Generator progress together in one file.
